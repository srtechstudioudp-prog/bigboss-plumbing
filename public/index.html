Why both under one roof

The slab and the pipes are one problem

Houston sits on clay that locals call gumbo. It swells when it is wet and shrinks when it dries. Anything that changes the moisture under a house, a leaking pipe included, changes how the slab sits.

  • →
    A leak can start a foundation problemWater under a slab softens soil and carries it away. The slab loses support and settles.
  • →
    Foundation movement can break pipesWhen a slab shifts, the lines running through it bend and crack, and a new leak begins.
  • →
    Fixing only one can leave the otherLifting a slab without checking the plumbing, or patching a pipe without checking the slab, can mean paying twice.
Warning signs

Read your house

You do not need to know what is wrong. If you see any of these, a quick look beats waiting. The tag shows which trade usually deals with it.

  • Cracks in walls, brick or the slab

    Stair-step cracks in brick and hairline cracks above doors are the usual first ones.

    Foundation

  • Doors and windows that stick

    They closed fine last year and now rub or will not latch. The frame has probably moved.

    Foundation

  • Floors that slope or bounce

    You can feel it in your feet, or see it where furniture sits crooked.

    Foundation

  • Gaps at walls, trim and ceilings

    Space opening up between walls, cabinets, door frames or crown moulding.

    Foundation

  • A water bill that jumped

    No change in how you live, but the bill went up. A hidden leak is a common cause.

    Plumbing

  • Damp spots, a musty smell, a warm patch

    A wet patch, a musty smell or a hot spot on the floor can come from a line you cannot see.

    Plumbing

The marble test. Set a marble or a ball on a hard floor. If it keeps rolling the same way, the floor is out of level. It is a rough check, but it tells you whether to call.

How do I know if my foundation needs repair?

Look for cracks in drywall, brick or the slab, doors and windows that stick, floors that slope, and gaps where walls meet ceilings or trim. One sign on its own can have other causes. Several together deserve a proper inspection.

Can a plumbing leak damage my foundation?

Yes. A leak under or beside a slab keeps the soil wet and can wash it away, and soil that moves lets the slab move with it. It also works the other way: a shifting foundation can bend or crack the pipes that run through it.

Do you handle both foundation and plumbing?

Yes. That is the point of the company. Houston homes tend to have both problems at once, so one team looks at the slab and the pipes together.

Do you work on commercial properties?

Yes. B&B Big Boss is listed for residential and commercial work, including commercial plumbing.

How much does foundation repair cost?

It depends on what is causing the movement, how much of the structure is affected and how hard it is to reach. We will not guess over the phone. After someone has looked at it, you get a clear scope and a price.

Take photos of the cracks or damage, note when you first saw them, and check your recent water bills for a jump. It helps us understand the problem before we arrive.
