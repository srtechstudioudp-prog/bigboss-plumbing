About

A Houston crew that does both jobs

B&B Big Boss Foundation Repair & Plumbing started in March 2022 and handles both trades, because the slab and the pipes are so often part of the same problem.

Why both trades

One team for the slab and the pipes

In Houston, homes sit on soil that moves, and the pipes run through the same slab that holds the house up. When something goes wrong with one, the other is often involved. Calling two companies means two visits, two opinions and sometimes two bills for the same root problem.

B&B Big Boss offers both trades. One team looks at the foundation and the plumbing together, explains what it found, and fixes what needs fixing.

The company works on homes and on commercial properties, and has been a BBB Accredited Business since March 2023. Reviewers on Google describe the team as professional, honest, reasonably priced and hard-working.
